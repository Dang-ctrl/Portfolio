CredMatch started as a hackathon question: *how much are people losing by using the wrong credit card?*

We built a recommendation engine that analyses spending patterns across categories — travel, dining, groceries and more — calculates the rewards you're leaving on the table, and suggests better alternatives.

## Takeaways

- A pitch is a story; the numbers are just proof
- Scope ruthlessly — a sharp demo beats a broad one
- Building under a clock teaches you what actually matters
