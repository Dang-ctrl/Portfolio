FarmHub lets farmers set their own prices and sell directly to buyers nearby — no middlemen.

- Real-time bidding over WebSockets
- Location-based matching between farmers and buyers
- A deliberately simple interface for first-time smartphone users

The hardest part wasn't the tech; it was deciding what to leave out.
